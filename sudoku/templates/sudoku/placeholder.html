How the algorithms work

Backtracking
A depth-first search. It fills the first empty cell, tries the digits 1–9, and reverses a choice as soon as it breaks a Sudoku rule. Simple and reliable, but it can explore many dead ends.
MRV (Minimum Remaining Values)
Same backtracking search, but it always fills the empty cell with the fewest legal candidates first. Doomed cells are noticed early, so far fewer dead ends are explored.
MRV + Forward Checking
MRV plus a look-ahead step: after each tentative placement it checks that no other empty cell has been left with zero candidates. Branches that would wipe out a cell are rejected immediately.

Fewer recursive calls do not always mean less wall-clock time: the heuristics spend extra work choosing cells and checking candidates. Compare the metrics rather than assuming one algorithm is always fastest.
